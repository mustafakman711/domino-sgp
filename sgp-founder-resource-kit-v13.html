Recent changes to check (October 2026)

  • EAEU medical devices: a protocol signed in December 2025 extended the transition. New national registrations are accepted until 31 December 2027, and only the Union route remains from 1 January 2028. Older guides still show 2026.
  • Uzbekistan medicines and devices: Cabinet Resolution 738 has governed registration since February 2026, and a new registration instruction covering dossier format and packaging came into force on 6 September 2026.
  • Uzbekistan digital health: Resolution 570 requires every medical and pharmacy organisation to connect to the national digital health platform and use e-prescriptions.
  • Uzbekistan personal data: Law 1125 (March 2026) limited mandatory in-country storage to biometric, genetic and telecom data. Genetic test results still have to stay in Uzbekistan.
  • AI rules: Kazakhstan's AI Law has applied since 18 January 2026, with risk classes, duties for high-risk systems and labelling of AI output. Uzbekistan added AI provisions and fines for unlawful AI processing of personal data in January 2026.
  • Kazakhstan personal data: amendments in force since 25 August 2026 add controller size classes, a register of data processors and new breach reporting rules.
  • Kazakhstan tax: the new Tax Code raised the standard VAT rate to 16% from 2026. Some older guides still show 12%.
  • Kyrgyzstan personal data: the Digital Code replaced the old Law on Personal Information on 8 February 2026.
  • IT Park Uzbekistan: residency rules in force since 1 April 2026 set an export revenue target of 20%, rising to 35% in 2027. Older tax benefit guides predate this.
  • Uzbekistan health insurance: from July 2026 private clinics can bill the state health insurance fund at uniform tariffs.
  • Patents: Uzbekistan is not a member of the Eurasian Patent Organization, so a Eurasian patent does not cover it. File nationally or through the PCT.
